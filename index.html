Overview

BS Digital and Social Media Marketing

Future-focused program designed to prepare graduates for leadership in an AI-driven, data-centric, and platform-based business environment.

Source: KBSBS Digital & Social Media Marketing04
Overview
  • Integrates strong marketing foundations with advanced digital strategy, social media intelligence, consumer behavior analytics, and AI-enabled marketing technologies
  • Students develop the ability to design, implement, and evaluate digital marketing initiatives using real-time data from social platforms, web analytics, e-commerce systems, and performance dashboards
  • Not a traditional marketing degree with digital add-ons, but a comprehensive digital-first program that equips students to operate at the intersection of strategy, analytics, technology, and creativity

DSMM 305

Consumer Behavior in the Digital Age

Pre-requisiteMKT 250Topics3
  • An overview of fundamental concepts of Consumer Behavior from the point-of-view of consumers living in a digital world
  • Interplay between technology, psychology, and consumer behavior, providing a comprehensive understanding of how individuals navigate the digital environment, make purchasing decisions, and interact with brands
  • Factors that shape consumer perceptions, attitudes, and motivations in the digital space, and how these insights can be leveraged to develop successful marketing strategies.

DSMM 330

Marketing Analytics & Computational Decision Making

Pre-requisiteMKT 250; STAT214Topics7
  • Marketing data ecosystem, data sources and structures, data extraction and cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and visualization
  • Defining and measuring marketing KPIs and performance metrics
  • Designing and interpreting A/B tests
  • Applying predictive analytics incl. regression & classification models for applications such as churn prediction
  • Customer segmentation and targeting, digital marketing analytics and attribution, product and pricing analytics, and dashboard design for data storytelling
  • Ethical considerations and data governance principles are integrated throughout.
  • Social Media Informatics — social network analysis, NLP-based sentiment and opinion mining, topic modeling, social listening and API data pipelines, multimodal content analytics, bot detection & GenAI-augmented analytics.

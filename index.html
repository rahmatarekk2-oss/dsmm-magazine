Overview

A future-focused marketing degree

The program prepares graduates for leadership in an AI-driven, data-centric and platform-based business environment.

It integrates strong marketing foundations with advanced digital strategy, social media intelligence, consumer behavior analytics and AI-enabled marketing technologies.

Students learn to design, implement and evaluate digital marketing initiatives using real-time data from social platforms, web analytics, e-commerce systems and performance dashboards.

Not a traditional marketing degree with digital add-ons, but a comprehensive digital-first program that equips students to operate at the intersection of strategy, analytics, technology and creativity.

What students will be able to do

Four learning objectives

01

Digital and social media marketing knowledge and research

Ability to explain and apply digital and social media marketing concepts, principles and theories, and to conduct market research.

02

Digital platforms and marketing technologies

Students will demonstrate the ability to explain, analyze and use digital platforms, marketing technologies and AI-enabled tools to support digital and social media marketing strategies.

03

Leadership and teamwork

Students will explain leadership principles, apply leadership skills and communicate effectively with teams addressing digital and social media marketing challenges.

04

Decision-making abilities

The ability to make informed business and ethical decisions.

DSMM benchmarked against international programs

What we took from five leading schools

Wharton (UPenn)

One bundled course: Digital Marketing, Social Media & E-Commerce, plus analytics electives.Anchor a rigorous analytics and e-commerce core course early in the sequence.

NUS Business School

BBA marketing specialization; Digital Marketing & E-Commerce module plus CRM, analytics and services modules.Pair the digital course with CRM and services so DSMM is not analytics-only.

NYU Stern

BS Business; one course: Social Media & Digital Marketing (TECH-UB 38).Social media as strategy belongs in its own course, not a footnote.

Aston University

BSc (Hons) Digital Marketing: content, UX, influencer, analytics, social selling, e-commerce; CIM; placement year.Template for a full standalone structure.

Manchester Met.

BSc (Hons) Digital Marketing built around AI: Fundamentals of Digital & AI Marketing, AI visibility, AI brand and content strategy; AACSB + EQUIS.Strongest match: AI-infused spine, responsible marketing and a final project capstone.

KFUPM DSMM

Standalone BSc: about 10 dedicated DSMM courses across two work-integrated tracks (DSMM-ST, DSMM-INT).Brings it together: elite academic rigor, a standalone structure and an AI spine.
